# Audit of the current site (mi-hotel.ca)

Reviewed 2026-10-26. Source: https://www.mi-hotel.ca/ and its Suites & Pricing and FAQ pages.

## Facts

- **Business:** MiHotel Inc, a cat boarding hotel with grooming. Opened 2024.
- **Address:** 40 Cowdray Court, Unit 2, Scarborough, ON M1S 1A1, Canada
- **Contact:** info@mi-hotel.ca, 647-526-6431
- **Hours (per site):** 7 days a week, 12:00–8:00 PM
- **Tagline:** "Perfect Tech For Purrfect Cat"
- **Socials:** @mihotel_ca on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok
- **Platform:** Wix
- **Booking:** a Google Form (https://forms.gle/dKkzFWrVB4PQ6GRm7)

## Positioning

The hotel sells itself on technology. Every suite has PetKit equipment: an automatic litter box, a smart feeder with a camera, and a filtered water fountain. Owners get 24/7 live camera access through an app and daily updates by text, email or Instagram.

## Pricing

| Suite | Daily | 7+ days | 28+ days | Extra cat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single Suite | $28 | $25 | $22 | Not allowed (one cat only) |
| Luxury Suite | $50 | $45 | $35 | +$10/day |
| Luxury Plus (22 sq ft) | $65 | $55 | $45 | +$10/day |

Grooming:
- **Basic:** bath and tidy, ear cleaning, nail trimming, teeth cleaning
- **Luxury:** show grooming, comb cut, lion cut
- Guests boarding at the hotel get 10% off grooming.

## FAQ content

- **Services:** short- and long-term boarding, feeding, playtime, grooming
- **Suite features:** 24/7 camera, plus live data from the litter box and feeder
- **Daily care:** feeding on the cat's own schedule, filtered water, litter cleaning, playtime
- **Updates:** daily, by text, email or Instagram
- **Special needs:** special diets and medications can be accommodated
- **Extensions:** possible if the hotel is told early enough

## Gaps and issues

1. **Cancellation policy:** the FAQ asks the question but gives no answer.
2. **Grooming prices:** not listed.
3. **Vaccination and health requirements:** not stated anywhere I could find.
4. **Hours conflict:** the site says 7 days a week, but a third-party directory (Leadstal) says Tuesday to Sunday.
5. **Suite sizes:** only Luxury Plus has a size listed.
6. **Booking:** the Google Form takes visitors off the site, can't check availability, and has no confirmation flow.
7. **Typo:** the Single Suite description says "accomondate".

These gaps are inputs for the rebuild. The booking system in particular is the main functional upgrade.
